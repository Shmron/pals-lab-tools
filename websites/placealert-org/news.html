Upcoming Podcast Series
CHEAQI-MNCH: Air Quality & Maternal, Newborn and Child Health
An upcoming podcast series built around our CHEAQI-MNCH project — Characterizing Effects of Air Quality in Maternal, Newborn and Child Health in Sub-Saharan Africa. Episodes will follow the science and the people behind the study: how we measure outdoor air pollution across The Gambia, Kenya and Mozambique, what road-traffic and PM2.5 exposure mean for mothers and babies, and how community, clinical and geospatial data come together to protect health in low-resource settings.
