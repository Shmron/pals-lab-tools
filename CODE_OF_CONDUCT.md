# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming and respectful for everyone, whatever their background, role or experience, and especially for researchers and trainees working in resource-constrained settings.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people working in a second language.
- Credit other people's work and data, and follow the licences and terms of use of data sources.
- Protect privacy. Never share personal or participant data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing private information, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately through GitHub (**Security** then **Report a vulnerability** is for security issues; for conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile). Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block accounts that break these expectations.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
